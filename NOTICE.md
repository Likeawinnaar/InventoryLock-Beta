InventoryLock is intended as a proprietary project. The original project code,
name, and original creative materials should not be reused or redistributed
without appropriate permission.

- **Public visibility is not confidentiality.** A public repository can be
  browsed, cloned, and forked under GitHub's platform terms.
- **A notice is not copy protection.** Copyright notices and a proprietary
  license do not make reverse engineering or copying technically impossible.
- **Original work only.** Third-party libraries, APIs, fonts, textures,
  screenshots and Minecraft itself have their own terms and rights.

  unreleased resources private. The copyright status of AI-generated imagery

- **Releases:** Publish only artifacts whose distribution rights have been
  checked. Do not publish secrets, credentials or personal information.
